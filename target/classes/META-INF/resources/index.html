Validation reference

Location rules

Location lookup is an internal gateway rather than a public endpoint. These cards show the immutable catalogue and calculate live utilization from active warehouses.

Rules applied during create and replacement

  • Location identifiers match exactly and are case-sensitive.
  • Active warehouse count cannot exceed the location limit.
  • Combined active capacity cannot exceed the location maximum.
  • Capacity must be positive and at least equal to stock.
  • Stock cannot be negative.
  • Replacement stock must exactly match predecessor stock.
